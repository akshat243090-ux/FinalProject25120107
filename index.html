About Me

I design with intent,
I build with care.

AV
6+ Years
of Craft

Hey — I'm Anika. I started designing websites as a teenager obsessing over beautiful blogs and broke portfolios, and never stopped.

Over six years, I've worked with fashion labels, fintech startups, restaurants, NGOs, and solo creatives. Every project taught me something about communication, restraint, and the power of a well-placed whitespace.

I believe the web should be beautiful AND fast. That a great homepage should explain what you do in under 5 seconds. That accessibility isn't optional, it's respect.

When I'm not designing, I'm reading design theory, hiking in the Aravalli hills, or making too much filter coffee.
